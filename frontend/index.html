METHODOLOGY

How StockSense is built

01

Market data

Daily OHLCV history across a selected universe of 30 liquid U.S. stocks, with SPY used for market-relative features.

02

Feature engineering

28 inputs covering returns, moving averages, volatility, volume, RSI, MACD, gaps, and relative performance.

03

Model comparison

Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Histogram Gradient Boosting were compared using chronological data.

04

Walk-forward testing

Each test period is evaluated only after training on earlier observations to better approximate unseen data.
